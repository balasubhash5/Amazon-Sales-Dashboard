# Amazon-Sales-Dashboard
Developed a Power BI Amazon Sales Dashboard to analyze sales, profits, shipment costs, and regional performance, identifying sales trends and category-wise insights through interactive visualizations.
 Project Overview

The Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides insights into sales performance, shipment costs, product quantities, profitability, discount trends, and regional sales distribution.

 Project Objectives

Analyze overall sales, shipment costs, and quantities sold.

Compare sales performance across product categories.

Track yearly sales trends and growth.

Evaluate profit and sales across different regions.

Analyze discounts across product sub-categories.

Identify state-wise profit distribution.

Compare segment-wise sales and profitability.

 Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization

Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

DAX – Calculated measures and KPIs

Data Visualization – Charts, tables, maps, and treemaps

 Dashboard Features

1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Sales: 2,297,200.86

Total Shipment Cost: 87.03K

Total Quantity: 38K

2. Sales by Category

Compares sales across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies to identify high-performing product categories.

3. Yearly Sales Analysis

Visualizes annual sales trends from 2014 to 2017 to understand changes in business performance.

4. Discount Analysis

Displays average discount percentages across product sub-categories to identify differences in discounting practices.

5. Category and Sub-Category Analysis

Uses a treemap to compare sales contributions from individual products and sub-categories.

6. Regional Sales and Profit

Compares sales and profit across the West, East, Central, and South regions.

7. State-Wise Profit Analysis

Uses a geographical map to visualize profit distribution across states in the United States.

8. Segment-Wise Performance

Compares sales and profit across Consumer and Corporate customer segments for different product categories.

 Key Insights

Technology records the highest sales among the three main product categories.

Yearly sales increase significantly from 2015 through 2017.

The West region has the highest sales among the four displayed regions.

Discount percentages vary considerably across product sub-categories.

The dashboard enables comparison of category, segment, and geographical performance.

 Business Benefits

Supports sales and profitability analysis.

Helps identify high-performing product categories.

Enables regional performance comparisons.

Provides insights into discounting patterns.

Supports informed business and sales planning.
